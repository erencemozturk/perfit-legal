1. Health and Safety Warning

PerFIT is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Nothing in the app — including workout suggestions, progress analyses, estimated strength calculations, muscle distribution charts, reminders and reports — replaces the examination, diagnosis or treatment of a physician, physiotherapist, dietitian or other qualified health professional.

Exercise carries a risk of injury. Weight training can lead to muscle, tendon, ligament and joint injuries, cardiovascular events and other health problems. This risk exists whether or not you use the app.

See a doctor before you start training. This is essential if you have a chronic condition, a past injury or cardiovascular disease, if you are pregnant, if you take medication or if you have been inactive for a long time.

You exercise at your own risk. The weight, rep, set and rest suggestions the app shows are statistical estimates derived from your past records; the app cannot know what you are able to lift on a given day. If you feel pain, dizziness, chest tightness, shortness of breath or any unusual symptom, stop immediately and get medical help.

Suggestions are rule-based. Smart Program adjustments, Monthly Report comments and Body Tracking comparisons are rule-based suggestions, not medical advice or a diagnosis. If you have an injury, pain or any other health problem, see a doctor.

1.1 About injury information

During first-time setup, PerFIT asks you a question about your injury history. Your answer is used only to personalize the content the app shows you. This is not a health assessment. PerFIT does not diagnose your injury, evaluate your recovery, offer a rehabilitation program, or determine whether an exercise is safe for your injury.

You can leave this question blank; the app still works.

1.2 Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, no liability is accepted for any injury, health problem or harm that arises during or as a result of using PerFIT. By using the app you acknowledge that you know this risk and that you exercise voluntarily and at your own responsibility.

This limitation applies subject to the mandatory provisions of the Turkish Code of Obligations; cases of liability that cannot be limited in advance by law are not limited by this text.

If you live in another country, including a member state of the European Union, this choice of law does not deprive you of the protection of the mandatory consumer laws of your country of residence, and you may also bring proceedings in the courts of your country of residence.

This text deliberately does not contain a jurisdiction clause tying disputes to the courts of a single city: in consumer contracts, such clauses restricting the consumer's right to apply to the court where they live are invalid.

For disputes with users who are not consumers, the general jurisdiction rules of the Code of Civil Procedure apply.
